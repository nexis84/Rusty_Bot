How the Career Program works

The AIR Career Program now lives inside Paragon's Achievement System.

The Career Program is the structured introduction to New Eden's play styles. It is split into four paths — Explorer, Enforcer, Industrialist and Soldier — with 78 achievements in total.

All capsuleers can take part, including characters who completed the old Career Program. Completing it again grants its rewards again. Achievements are now found in the NEOCOM.

78 achievements

14 Explorer, 14 Enforcer, 24 Industrialist and 26 Soldier achievements.

Four paths

Each path tracks a different play style and has its own milestones and rewards.

Repeatable rewards

All capsuleers can complete the program, and repeat completions grant the rewards again.

FAQ

How many AIR Career achievements are there?

78, split across Explorer (14), Enforcer (14), Industrialist (24) and Soldier (26).

Are these all the achievements in the game?

No. The client also has Factional Warfare, Combat, Exploration, Industry and Other categories, plus time-limited campaign and Path to Conquest achievements.

Do I have to complete the paths in order?

No. Each achievement tracks its own progress. The paths group the content and each has its own milestones.

Can I complete the Career Program again?

Yes. All capsuleers can take part, including characters who completed the old program, and completing it again grants its rewards again.

Where are achievements in the UI?

The Career Program is now in the NEOCOM's Paragon Achievement System.

What are Discoverable achievements?

Achievements that hide their exact requirements. Their name, rewards and flavor text provide hints.

What are the early-game achievements?

Academy Pilot, Stellar Exploration, Home of High-Sec, Tour the Embassy and Report For Duty.

Why do some descriptions show a higher target?

Many achievements have several tiers. The description uses the highest tier for readability; the exact tier values are listed underneath each entry.

Where did this list come from?

The EVE client's achievement data and English localization, cross-checked against the Fractured Skies expansion notes on 10 November 2026.
