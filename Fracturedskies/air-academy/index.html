What is AIR Academy?

AIR Academy Ships are free, pre-fitted ships available at stations throughout Exordium. They let new capsuleers experiment with different ships, weapons and activities without needing to master the fitting system first.

The first selection contains 13 ships. Each fitting introduces the weapons, defenses, ranges and playstyle associated with its faction and role. Ships from every faction can be claimed regardless of your character's faction.

Designed exclusively for Exordium. Academy Ships operate in Exordium and the Abyss, with travel and economy restrictions covered below.

Claiming & switching

Visit a station in Exordium and open the AIR Academy Ships service. You can hold one Academy Ship at a time.

  1. 1
    Open AIR Academy Ships

    Visit a station in Exordium and open the AIR Academy Ships service.

  2. 2
    Accept the conditions

    Accept the conditions of use before claiming your first ship.

  3. 3
    Claim one ship

    You can have one Academy Ship at a time.

  4. 4
    Switching ships

    Claiming a new Academy Ship removes the previous ship and its supplied Academy equipment. Player-added cargo and standard equipment are returned to your inventory.

  5. 5
    First-claim animation

    The first claim of each ship type plays a special introduction animation.

Good to know

  • One Academy Ship at a time.
  • Any faction can claim any Academy Ship, regardless of character faction.
  • Your first claim of each ship type has a special animation.
  • Claiming a new ship returns your added cargo and standard equipment.

Fitting & skills

Academy Ships use a mix of Academy and standard equipment, with a few restrictions.

  • Academy Ships can use both Academy and standard equipment.
  • Academy modules can only be fitted to Academy Ships.
  • Academy ammunition can only be loaded into Academy modules.
  • Academy drones can only be used by Academy Ships.
  • Skills that improve standard ships and equipment also improve their Academy versions.
  • Academy Ships, modules and charges cannot be traded or reprocessed.
Your skills still matter. Anything that improves the standard version of a ship or module also improves its Academy counterpart.

Travel & economy restrictions

Academy Ships are designed for Exordium and the Abyss. They are not normal market ships.

Where you can fly

  • Academy Ships can only operate in Exordium and the Abyss.
  • They cannot leave Exordium through the Yulai stargate or the Shipcaster routes to Mondaekus, Ichorah, Dettisolin or Tatrys.
  • Outside Exordium and the Abyss, they cannot undock or warp.
  • Players cannot eject from an Academy Ship.
  • Blocked travel displays an explanation and an option to purchase the equivalent standard ship and fitting.

Economy restrictions

  • Academy Ships, modules and charges cannot be traded or reprocessed.
  • Academy Ships and modules cannot be repackaged.
  • They cannot be stored in ship maintenance bays, ship cargo holds or containers.
  • Academy Ship wrecks do not provide salvage.
These restrictions apply to the Academy versions. Standard ships, modules and cargo are unaffected.

From Academy to standard

When you find a ship you like, you can buy the standard version and fitting without losing your Academy Ship.

Buy Standard Version

  • Opens Multibuy with the standard versions of the selected ship, fitting, ammunition and drones.
  • Standard market prices, availability and purchase rules apply.
  • Buying the standard version does not remove your Academy Ship.

AIR Academy Ship Tree

  • Ships with an AIR Academy version are highlighted in the Ship Tree.
  • The Academy Ships interface includes a shortcut to the relevant Ship Tree view.
  • You can compare Academy Ships with their standard counterparts.
  • Hovering over a ship displays additional information in the standard tooltip.

AIR Career Program

78 new achievements

The AIR Career Program moves into Paragon's Achievement System. All capsuleers can take part and complete it, including those who completed it before — completing it again grants all its rewards.

  • Achievements are now in the NEOCOM.
  • New Discoverable achievements hide their exact requirements but offer hints through their name, rewards and flavor text.
  • Four Discoverable achievements launch with the expansion.
  • New early-game achievements reward new capsuleers for exploring and learning.

FAQ

What are AIR Academy Ships?

Free, pre-fitted ships available at stations throughout Exordium. They let new capsuleers try different ships and activities without needing to master fitting first.

How many ships are there?

13: eight combat ships, four exploration ships and one mining ship.

Can I keep more than one?

No. You can have one Academy Ship at a time. Claiming a new one removes the previous ship and its supplied Academy equipment.

Can I use my own modules?

Yes, Academy Ships can use both Academy and standard equipment. Academy modules, ammunition and drones are restricted to Academy Ships.

Where can I fly them?

Only in Exordium and the Abyss. They cannot leave Exordium through the Yulai stargate or the Shipcaster routes to Mondaekus, Ichorah, Dettisolin or Tatrys.

Can I sell or store them?

No. Academy Ships, modules and charges cannot be traded, reprocessed, repackaged or stored in ship maintenance bays, cargo holds or containers. Their wrecks do not provide salvage.

How do I move to a standard ship?

Use “Buy Standard Version” on the Academy Ship. It opens Multibuy with the standard ship, fitting, ammunition and drones. Buying the standard version does not remove your Academy Ship.

What are the new achievements?

The AIR Career Program adds 78 achievements and moves into Paragon's Achievement System. All capsuleers can complete it, including repeat completions for rewards. Early-game examples include Academy Pilot, Stellar Explorations, Home of High-Sec, Tour the Embassy and Report for Duty.

Do Academy Ships appear in the Ship Tree?

Yes. Ships with an Academy version are highlighted, and you can compare them with the standard hulls or open the relevant Ship Tree view from the Academy Ships interface.
