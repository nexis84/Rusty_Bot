What is EVE Vanguard?

EVE Vanguard is the ground-combat side of New Eden. On 10 November 2026 it becomes playable 24/7 as a paid Alpha alongside Fractured Skies, with Founder's Access purchased through the EVE launcher.

Warclones take on contracts and expeditions for the four empires. Their combined efforts connect directly to the war in EVE Online — unlocking sites, feeding campaigns and, from February, contributing Conquest Power.

24/7 paid Alpha

Playable around the clock from 10 November 2026, not just during limited playtest windows.

Warclone contracts

Take contracts for the four empires, complete expeditions and advance your warclone.

Permanent connection

Ground actions feed the war in space. The link follows the Operation Nemesis and Operation Avalon playtests.

What we know so far: CCP has announced the Alpha, the new maps and the connection to EVE. Exact Founder's Access pricing, the full progression numbers and the complete Alpha ruleset have not been published.

The three battlegrounds

Three new maps with different security levels, each built around a different kind of fight.

High Castle

Highsec

A siege-like fortress where you can operate without PvP damage.

  • Deploy solo or with a squad.
  • Tackle shared objectives.
  • No PvP damage.

Vorago

Lowsec

A hostile chasm where enemy forces and rival warclones stand between you and extraction.

  • Fight through hostile terrain.
  • Fight for your haul.
  • Try to make it out alive.

The Vault

Nullsec

A deadly PvP labyrinth that pits three squads of three against one another.

  • The layout changes between deployments.
  • Extract rare Weapon Cores for high-tier weapons.
  • Only one squad makes it out with the prize.

Progression & combat

The November update expands progression, equipment and the new-player journey.

Weapon Telemetry

Gain experience with a weapon family to earn Weapon Telemetry, unlocking stronger variants and new loadout options.

New equipment & BR-60

New equipment joins the arsenal, including the Gallente BR-60. Deeper weapon progression opens more loadout choices.

New-player journey

An improved new-player journey and a refreshed combat experience make the Alpha easier to approach.

Ground war, space consequences

Vanguard is not a separate game. Ground actions unlock opportunities in New Eden.

Contracts & expeditions

Warclones complete contracts and expeditions for the four empires. Their combined efforts unlock additional combat, hacking and mining sites across highsec and lowsec.

  • Capsuleers can enter these sites for valuable rewards.
  • Rewards include items that contribute to Military Campaigns.

SARO Black Sites

During the new Military Campaigns, Vanguard players completing expeditions and contracts spawn temporary SARO Black Sites.

  • PvE, mining and exploration sites in highsec and lowsec.
  • More campaign-related drops than regular sites.
  • Short-lived; check the Theaters of War section or the Activity map filter.
From February 2027: Vanguard expeditions contribute Conquest Power to the chosen side, making the ground war part of the race for the final Siege.

FAQ

What is EVE Vanguard?

The ground-combat side of New Eden. Warclones take on contracts and expeditions for the four empires, with actions that connect back to EVE Online.

When does the Alpha start?

10 November 2026, alongside the Fractured Skies expansion. It becomes playable 24/7 as a paid Alpha.

Is it free?

No. EVE Vanguard enters paid Alpha with Founder's Access. CCP has not yet published the pricing.

How do I get Founder's Access?

Founder's Access is purchased through the EVE launcher from 10 November. CCP said more details on pricing and access would follow.

What are the new maps?

High Castle (highsec siege fortress), Vorago (lowsec hostile chasm) and The Vault (nullsec 3v3v3 PvP labyrinth).

What is Weapon Telemetry?

Experience earned with a weapon family. It unlocks stronger variants and new loadout options.

What is the BR-60?

The Gallente BR-60 weapon joining the expanding Vanguard arsenal in the November update.

How does Vanguard affect EVE Online?

Warclone contracts and expeditions unlock combat, hacking and mining sites in highsec and lowsec. During Military Campaigns they spawn SARO Black Sites with extra campaign drops. From February 2027, expeditions also contribute Conquest Power.

What are SARO Black Sites?

Temporary PvE, mining and exploration sites that spawn during Military Campaigns when Vanguard players complete expeditions and contracts. They offer more campaign-related drops than regular sites but are short-lived.

What is still unknown?

Exact Founder's Access pricing, the full progression numbers and equipment list, Alpha duration and the complete ruleset. CCP has not published those details yet.
