The full FW change list from the Fractured Skies expansion notes.

FW changes at a glance

Finite complexes, better tag drops and new loot chances.

Five waves

NPCs in FW complexes are capped at five waves instead of respawning infinitely in capture complexes.

More tags

The drop rate of NPC tags from FW NPCs is greatly increased.

SKINR & boosters

FW NPCs can drop SKINR components again, and all FW NPCs can drop boosters from the current campaign.

Haulers & bosses

FW mission haulers now drop tags, and boss NPCs such as the Field Commander drop more.

The goal: make FW complexes worth completing, not just farming infinite respawns. Fewer waves, but better loot per NPC.

Complex NPCs capped at 5 waves

NPCs in FW complexes are now limited to five waves, instead of respawning infinitely in capture complexes.

That gives a complex a finite NPC population. You get incremental value while completing it, rather than an endless spawn cycle.

Capture complexes are the specific example in the notes. They previously respawned NPCs indefinitely; now the NPC waves are capped.

Tag drops are up

More tags from regular FW NPCs, haulers and bosses.

FW NPCs Drop rate

Regular NPC tags

  • The drop rate of NPC tags from FW NPCs is greatly increased.

Bottom line: more tag income from the NPCs you already kill in FW complexes.

FW missions Haulers

Haulers drop tags

  • Haulers in FW missions now drop tags when they are destroyed.

Bottom line: the hauler in an FW mission is now a loot target, not just an objective.

FW missions Boss NPCs

Boss NPC tags

  • The amount of tags dropped by boss NPCs, such as the Field Commander, is increased.

Bottom line: boss NPCs are worth more of your time.

Income What it means

More value per complex

  • Fewer NPC respawns, but more tags per NPC.
  • Haulers and boss NPCs are priority loot targets.

Bottom line: clear the five waves and loot everything — the notes say the loot per NPC is better.

No exact rates are published. The notes say the drop rate and boss amounts increased, but not by how much.

SKINR components

FW NPCs once again have a small chance to drop SKINR components.

The notes do not list which components, the drop rate, or which NPC types can drop them. Treat it as an extra roll on top of the tag drops.

Campaign boosters

All FW NPCs now have a small chance to drop boosters from the current campaign.

This ties FW NPC farming to the active Military Campaign. The notes do not list the booster types or the drop chance.

Check the current campaign: the booster pool follows whichever Military Campaign is active, so its value can change over time.

What it means

FW complexes now have a finite NPC population, but each NPC is worth more. The notes describe this as a trade: fewer respawns, better loot per kill, and incremental value while completing a complex.

“We're limiting the number of times NPCs respawn in FW complexes. This means we don't have to be as conservative with their loot drop values, and can increase the amount of loot they drop. It also means players get incremental value while completing FW complexes.”

EVE Online Team — Fractured Skies expansion notes
Practical effect: clear the five waves, loot the tags, and check for SKINR components or campaign boosters. Haulers and boss NPCs are priority loot targets.

FAQ

What changed in FW complexes?

NPCs are now limited to five waves instead of respawning infinitely in capture complexes.

Do NPCs still respawn?

Up to five waves. The endless respawn in capture complexes is gone.

Did tag drops increase?

Yes. The drop rate of NPC tags from FW NPCs is greatly increased.

Do haulers drop tags?

Yes. Haulers in FW missions now drop tags when destroyed.

Do boss NPCs drop more tags?

Yes. Boss NPCs such as the Field Commander drop an increased amount of tags.

Can FW NPCs drop SKINR components?

Yes. They have a small chance to drop SKINR components again.

Can FW NPCs drop boosters?

Yes. All FW NPCs have a small chance to drop boosters from the current campaign.

What does this mean for FW income?

Fewer NPC respawns, but better loot per NPC: more tags, plus small chances at SKINR components and campaign boosters.

What is still unknown?

Exact tag drop rates, tag values, which SKINR components and boosters can drop, and the exact boss NPC amounts.
