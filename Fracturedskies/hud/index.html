What's changing

The first iteration focuses on the command board — the center-bottom cluster that holds your capacitor, modules, shield, and armor. CCP's stated aims:

Module states & ship status

Clearer communication of what your modules are doing and what's happening to your ship.

Danger, at a glance

More recognizable danger indicators, and better prioritisation of information that needs attention now.

Easier to discover

Overheating, precise speed controls, and other features should be easier to find and understand.

Clarity for everyone

More clarity for new players; more precision and insight for experienced pilots.

Why now

The HUD has been part of EVE for two decades. Much of its current form dates to around 2005, and for many players it is the only HUD they have ever known. It is iconic — but it was not designed for the way people learn games today.

After Photon UI, CCP is taking a deliberate look at the command board. The intention is not to make EVE simpler, or to change it just to look new and shiny. It is to improve telegraphing, legibility, and clarity while preserving the identity of EVE's HUD.

“The goal is not to make EVE simpler, but to make its complexity easier to engage with.”

EVE Online Team

Evolution images: CCP Games / Fenris Creations, via the EVE Evolved dev blog.

The design principles

Telegraphing

Danger should be easier to recognize. Important information should announce itself before it becomes a problem.

Legibility

Status, states, and priorities should be readable at a glance — not learned by osmosis.

Clarity

Moment-to-moment piloting should feel natural, without removing the depth that makes EVE, EVE.

Preserve the identity. CCP is explicitly trying to keep the HUD unmistakably EVE. The goal is not a simpler game; it is complexity that is easier to engage with.

Designed with players

A design is a hypothesis. CCP tested this one with new players, the CSM, and players at Fanfest.

New-player interviews & usability sessions

Watching where the current HUD creates friction, and how new designs communicate important information.

CSM reviews

Concepts and iterations reviewed with the Council of Stellar Management across playstyles.

Fanfest workshop

A dedicated HUD workshop and UI/UX booth feedback from players at Fanfest.

“A design is ultimately a hypothesis about what will work best for the people using it.”

EVE Online Team

How to enable the New Ship HUD

  1. 1
    Open Settings

    Press Esc in the EVE client.

  2. 2
    Go to Feature Previews

    It is in the left sidebar.

  3. 3
    Select New Ship HUD

    The preview is marked NEW.

  4. 4
    Enable it

    No restart required. Toggle it off any time.

What exactly is the command board?

The center-bottom part of the HUD. It holds the capacitor, module rack, shield, and armor information — the cluster CCP is focused on for this first iteration.

Is the new HUD mandatory?

No. It is an opt-in Feature Preview right now. CCP plans to move to an opt-out beta later, then make the HUD the default once it has been refined with player feedback.

Will it change how the game plays?

No. It is a UI update. The underlying game systems, rules, and balance are unchanged.

Does it apply to structure HUDs?

Not yet. The current preview applies to ship HUDs only; structure control HUDs remain unchanged at this time.

How do I enable it?

Open Settings, go to Feature Previews, select New Ship HUD, and enable it. No restart is required, and you can toggle it off at any time. See the walkthrough above.

Where does feedback go?

Use the in-client feedback tools and CCP's official channels. CCP says feedback from the feature preview will be used to improve future versions.
