What changed

The modernization touches visuals, the overview and the underlying site data.

37 sites updated

CCP updated the visuals and clarity of 37 Level 1 combat mission sites.

Scenery is non-interactable

Scenery objects are now non-interactable, reducing overview clutter and the number of brackets displayed in space.

Overseer Structure group

Mission completion objects now use the Overseer Structure group, so they appear on the overview by default.

Triggers cleaned up

Mission triggers and site data have been cleaned up in the updated sites.

The big quality-of-life win is the overview: fewer scenery brackets and a completion object that is visible without adding extra overview settings.

Why it matters

Level 1 missions are the first PvE many players see. Cleaner sites and a clearer overview reduce noise and make the objective easier to find.

Less overview noise

Non-interactable scenery no longer fills the overview with brackets, so the important entries stand out.

Easier objectives

Completion objects are in the Overseer Structure group and appear by default, making the next step clearer.

Updated visuals

The selected sites have refreshed scenery and cleaner site data without changing the core mission idea.

FAQ

What is the Level 1 Mission Modernization?

A visual and clarity pass over 37 Level 1 combat mission sites, including cleaner scenery, a clearer overview and updated site data.

How many sites changed?

37 Level 1 combat mission sites.

What changed in the overview?

Scenery objects are now non-interactable, which reduces overview clutter and the number of brackets in space.

Why are completion objects easier to find?

They are now in the Overseer Structure group, so they appear on the overview by default.

Were mission triggers changed?

Yes. Mission triggers and site data have been cleaned up in the updated sites.

Do the missions have new objectives?

The notes focus on visuals, overview clarity and site cleanup. They do not describe new mission objectives.

Are all Level 1 missions updated?

CCP lists 37 updated sites. The mission table above shows the named missions and variants in the notes.

Do I need to do anything to see the changes?

No. The updated sites are the live mission sites after the expansion.
