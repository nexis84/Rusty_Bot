Military Campaigns

Four empire campaigns, four objectives, and a race to shape the next stage of the war.

Read expansion notes Back to launch hub
Launch 10 Nov 2026 Bonuses 5 Jan 2027 1.2M SP per campaign

Theaters of War campaign briefing (pre-launch capture). Image: CCP Games / Fenris Creations.

How they work

Each campaign has four objectives, all live at launch. Complete activities, deliver the items, and push your empire forward.

Four objectives, live now

All four objectives are available from launch; no new objectives appear over the course of the campaign.

Personal = community

Your personal contribution can match the community's progress. There is no per-character cap until the objective is complete.

Deliver to the War HQ

Collect faction-specific items and deliver them to the item trader in your empire's War HQ system.

Race for the finish

The first empire to complete all four objectives earns command carrier reinforcements in the January showdown.

The four campaigns

Choose an empire, deliver to its War HQ, and race its rivals to complete all four objectives. The commander listed below attends the January battlefield if that empire finishes first.

Activities that count

Campaign progress comes from exploration, PvE, PvP and industry. Deliver the resulting items to your empire's War HQ.

Ambushed expeditions

Complete ambushed expedition PvE sites, destroy enemy NPCs, then salvage the expedition ships to recover prototypes.

  • Designed for a well-fit Assault Frigate or Navy Destroyer.
  • Highest concentration in Bulwark and FW systems.

Mining

Mine ore in Bulwark systems, or richer ore in FW frontline systems, then reprocess it and deliver the materials to the item trader.

  • Deliver to the War HQ item trader.
  • Frontline ore is richer than Bulwark ore.

Hacking Drifter caches

Hack Collapsed Drifter Caches and Fabricated Rogue Caches in highsec, lowsec and C1–C3 wormhole space.

  • Engineering section — Amarr data.
  • Logistics section — Caldari data.
  • Communications section — Minmatar data.

FW complexes

Destroy NPCs in regular Factional Warfare capture complexes, recover strategic intel, and deliver it to your empire.

  • FW NPCs now have increased tag drop rates.
  • Complex NPCs are limited to five waves.
War HQs: Amarr — Mehatoor · Caldari — Onnamon · Gallente — Intaki · Minmatar — Amo. Deliver campaign items to the item trader in your chosen empire's War HQ.

Why it matters

The campaigns are a race with consequences that outlast launch. Every completed objective strengthens an empire; the first to finish all four gains an edge in the January showdown.

The campaigns also feed the wider war: the bonuses activate in January, the empires clash over Drifter technology, and the first Conquests arrive in February.

“Every completed campaign objective unlocks a bonus for that empire, and the first of each opposing rival faction to complete all their objectives earns special command carrier reinforcements in an upcoming empire showdown.”

FAQ

What are Military Campaigns?

Four empire campaigns, each with four objectives. Complete activities, deliver the items to your empire's War HQ, and earn personal rewards while progressing the campaign.

When do they start?

With the Fractured Skies expansion on 10 November 2026.

Do I need to be in Factional Warfare?

The expansion notes do not list a Factional Warfare enlistment requirement. You contribute by completing the campaign activities and delivering the items to the item trader in your chosen empire's War HQ.

How do I contribute?

Run ambushed expedition sites, mine in Bulwark or FW frontline systems, hack Drifter and Rogue caches, or fight in FW complexes. Then deliver the resulting items to your empire's War HQ.

What are the rewards?

Each campaign has six regular and six hidden achievements. Regular achievements grant 5 Penumbral Shadows SKINs, 6 titles and 600,000 SP; hidden achievements grant 6 hidden titles and another 600,000 SP. That is 1.2 million SP per campaign, plus ISK, LP and faction loyalty tokens from item hand-ins.

When do the campaign bonuses activate?

From 5 January 2027, the bonuses become active in solar systems under the sovereign control of the faction, and in any territory it occupies through Factional Warfare at that time.

What happens in January?

The four empires clash in a major lowsec battle over access to Drifter technology. The winning empire gains access to an exclusive Drifter module through its LP store for a limited time.

Where are the War HQs?

Amarr — Mehatoor · Caldari — Onnamon · Gallente — Intaki · Minmatar — Amo.
