The four hulls

All four are available as blueprint copies from Factional Warfare LP stores for 52,000 LP and 13 million ISK each.

Amarr

Amarr Navy Battlecruiser

Large Energy Turrets · optimal-range platform

  • 15% bonus to Large Energy Turret damage
  • 7.5% bonus to Large Energy Turret optimal range

  • 95% reduction in Large Energy Turret powergrid requirement
  • 50% reduction in Large Energy Turret CPU requirement
  • 50% reduction in Large Energy Turret activation cost

Why fly it: the range option. Keep at the edge of the fight and apply beam damage from farther out.

How to get them

The hulls are built from blueprint copies sold in the four militia LP stores.

1

Enlist in FW

Join a Factional Warfare militia for the empire whose hull you want.

2

Earn LP

Run FW complexes, missions and Military Campaigns to build up Loyalty Points.

3

Buy the BPC

Spend 52,000 LP and 13 million ISK in your militia's LP store.

4

Build the hull

Manufacture the ship from the blueprint copy, then fit it like any other battlecruiser.

The LP stores sell blueprint copies, not assembled ships. You still need industry and the usual materials to build the hull.

Why it matters

Attack battlecruisers live on the edge between overwhelming firepower and vulnerability. The navy variants do not remove that trade-off — they give each empire a new way to exploit it.

For smaller forces, the appeal is the same as always: hit above your weight, then get out before the hammer falls. The Naga leans into missiles, the Tornado into projectiles and MWD mobility, the Oracle into laser range, and the Talos into hybrid damage and falloff.

“Attack battlecruisers have always lived on the edge between overwhelming firepower and vulnerability. These navy variants create new opportunities for capsuleers willing to exploit that balance, particularly when smaller forces need to hit above their weight.”

EVE Online Team — Fractured Skies expansion article

FAQ

What are the four navy attack battlecruisers?

Oracle Navy Issue (Amarr), Naga Navy Issue (Caldari), Talos Navy Issue (Gallente) and Tornado Fleet Issue (Minmatar).

How do I get them?

Join a Factional Warfare militia, earn LP, and buy the blueprint copy from your militia's LP store for 52,000 LP and 13 million ISK. Then build the hull from the BPC.

Do I need to be in Factional Warfare?

Yes — the BPCs are sold in the militia LP stores, so you need access to the relevant Factional Warfare LP store.

Which one should I fly?

Pick by weapon system and role: Naga for missiles, Tornado for projectiles and MWD mobility, Oracle for laser range, Talos for hybrid damage and falloff.

Are they better than the base hulls?

They are different tools, not automatic upgrades. The navy variants change the bonuses and role — check the bonuses above and decide what fits your fleet or playstyle.

Where is the official source?

The Fractured Skies expansion notes, under New Ships.
