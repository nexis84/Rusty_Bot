Balance at a glance

The headline changes, before you dive into the numbers.

Carriers

Light Fighter application returns through the Integrated Sensor Array active effect. Tech II Heavy Fighters also gain 2–10% damage from Fighter Specialization skills.

Navy Fighters

Templar, Firbolg and Equite get more shield HP, damage, speed and sensor strength. Their charge count and rearm time also improve.

Strategic Cruisers

Legion and Proteus hulls improve, Tengu and Proteus subsystems get utility, and the Loki gets a deliberately small powergrid bump.

Defenders & bombs

Defender Launchers cost just 5 GJ, Defender Missiles fly for 5 seconds, and Void Bombs are slower with a 12-second fuse.

The theme: carriers must commit to the field, Navy Fighters become more viable, underused hulls get help, and bombs get more counterplay without a direct damage nerf.

Carriers & Fighters

Carrier application returns, and the Navy fighter line gets a broad buff.

Heavy Fighters Skill requirement

Tech II Heavy Fighters

  • Now require the Fighter Specialization skills added in the Catalyst Major Update.
  • Gain 2%–10% more damage, depending on the skill level trained.

Bottom line: training the specialization skills is now part of using Tech II Heavy Fighters well.

Integrated Sensor Array Active effect

Light Fighter application

  • +20% Light Fighter Attack Explosion Velocity.
  • −10% Light Fighter Attack Explosion Radius.
  • Not penalized when stacked with Omnidirectional Tracking Links or Enhancers.

Bottom line: the ISA active effect restores much of the Light Fighter application lost in 2019, but the carrier has to commit the module.

Navy Light Attack Templar · Firbolg

More HP, damage and charges

  • Shield HP: 5,445 → 7,260.
  • Heavy Rocket Salvo charges: 20 → 24.
  • Rearm time: 4s → 3s per charge.
  • More primary weapon and salvo damage, plus higher speed and sensor strength.

Bottom line: Navy attack fighters last longer and apply more damage between reloads.

Navy Space Superiority Equite

Tougher anti-fighter platform

  • Shield HP: 2,640 → 3,520.
  • Micro Missile Swarm damage: 40 → 42.
  • Max velocity: 1,040 → 1,092 m/s.
  • Radar sensor strength: 8 → 12.

Bottom line: the Equite is tougher and faster, with a little more punch.

Strategic Cruisers & Subsystems

The least-used T3C hulls and subsystems get help. The Loki gets a deliberately smaller boost.

The notes describe all four T3Cs as improvements, but the Loki is already the most-used PvP ship in the game, so CCP kept its change conservative.

Why the Loki gets less: it has the highest damage on solo and small-gang player ship kills and ranks high on total killmail damage. The other three T3Cs were the priority.

HACs, Battlecruisers & Frigates

Small improvements for underused hulls, plus corrective changes for the Sacrilege and Hawk.

Battlecruiser Myrmidon Navy Issue

Myrmidon Navy Issue

  • Maximum capacitor: 2,900 → 3,000.

Bottom line: a small capacitor bump for the least-used Navy combat battlecruiser.

Heavy Assault Cruiser Sacrilege

Sacrilege

  • HAC bonus to missile launcher rate of fire: 7.5% → 5% per level.
  • Amarr Cruiser bonus to HAM and Heavy Missile damage: 5% → 7.5% per level.
  • Effective launchers: 10 → 9.17.

Bottom line: a correction to the Cradle of War buff — still stronger than before that update, just less buffy.

Heavy Assault Cruiser Cerberus

Cerberus

  • The 2.5% all-missile damage bonus is split.
  • Kinetic missile damage: 2.5% → 5%.
  • EM, Thermal and Explosive: stays 2.5%.

Bottom line: more damage when using kinetic missiles, while keeping the Cerberus' damage-type versatility.

Destroyer Catalyst

Catalyst

  • Maximum velocity: 265 → 275 m/s.

Bottom line: a small ship-vs-ship boost to help it stay in range, without changing its ganking profile.

Frigate Maulus Navy Issue

Maulus Navy Issue

  • Armor HP: 525 → 625.
  • Structure HP: 550 → 575.
  • Total base HP: 1,600, matching the Crucifier Navy Issue and Griffin Navy Issue.

Bottom line: a durability bump to bring it in line with the other Navy electronic-attack frigates.

Frigate Vengeance

Vengeance

  • Base capacitor recharge time: 187.5 → 140.625s.
  • Removed: 5% capacitor recharge rate per Assault Frigate level.
  • Added: 3% armor HP per Assault Frigate level.
  • Shield HP 664 → 464; armor HP 816 → 1,016.

Bottom line: the old cap-recharge bonus is built into the hull, and the Vengeance shifts further toward armor.

Frigate Hawk

Hawk

  • Removed: 7.5% bonus to rocket and light missile damage.
  • Added: 10% kinetic rocket and light missile damage.
  • Added: 5% EM, Thermal and Explosive rocket and light missile damage.

Bottom line: kinetic damage is restored, and the Hawk keeps better damage-type versatility than before the Major Update.

Expedition Frigate Odysseus

Odysseus

  • The Expedition Hold can now store drones.

Bottom line: a quality-of-life fix for the Odysseus' expedition storage.

Defenders & Bombs

More counterplay to bombs without directly reducing bomb damage.

Defender Launchers Capacitor cost

Defender Launchers

  • Capacitor cost to activate: 50 → 5 GJ.

Bottom line: much easier for destroyers to use, especially with lower capacitor skills.

Defender Missiles Flight time

Defender Missiles

  • Maximum flight time: 3 → 5 seconds.
  • Effective range: roughly 30–45km → 50–75km.

Bottom line: more consistent interception, and new players with Defender Launchers trained to level 1 can do the job.

Void Bomb Explosion delay

Void Bomb

  • Explosion delay: 7.5 → 12 seconds.
  • Maximum velocity: 4,000 → 2,500 m/s.
  • Focused Void Bomb is unchanged.

Bottom line: matches damage bombs, giving targets more time to move, firewall or use Defender Launchers.

CONCORD Beacon Deployment distance

Rogue Analysis Beacon

  • Minimum deployment distance from starbases: 30,000 → 50,000km.
  • Applies to the regular and CONCORD-Carrier versions.

Bottom line: beacons must be deployed farther from structures.

Bombs still hit hard. The change is about reaction time: Defenders are cheaper and longer-ranged, and Void Bombs are slower with a longer fuse.

What it means

The balance pass is not one big swing; it is a set of targeted changes. Carriers trade safety for application, Navy Fighters become more than a niche option, and the least-used T3Cs get quality-of-life improvements.

The Sacrilege and Hawk changes are corrections rather than removals. The Vengeance keeps its capacitor strength but becomes more clearly an armor ship. Defenders and Void Bombs get more counterplay without a direct damage reduction.

FAQ

What is the headline change?

Carriers get Light Fighter application back through the Integrated Sensor Array active effect, and Navy Fighters receive broad HP, damage and speed buffs.

Do Tech II Heavy Fighters need a new skill?

Yes. They now require the Fighter Specialization skills added in the Catalyst Major Update. Training them adds 2%–10% damage, depending on the level.

Which T3C changed the most?

The Legion and Proteus get hull improvements, while the Tengu and Proteus get subsystem utility. The Loki gets a deliberately small powergrid bump.

Is the Sacrilege nerfed?

It is a correction to the Cradle of War buff. Effective launchers drop from 10 to 9.17, but it is still stronger than before that update.

What changed for the Hawk?

The flat 7.5% rocket and light missile damage bonus is replaced by 10% kinetic damage and 5% EM, Thermal and Explosive damage. Kinetic damage is restored, and the Hawk keeps better damage-type versatility.

What changed for the Vengeance?

Its capacitor recharge is built into the hull, the old per-level capacitor bonus is removed, and it gains 3% armor HP per Assault Frigate level. Some hitpoints move from shield to armor.

How did Void Bombs change?

Explosion delay increased from 7.5 to 12 seconds and maximum velocity dropped from 4,000 to 2,500 m/s. The Focused Void Bomb is unchanged.

Did Defender Launchers get cheaper?

Yes. Activation cost dropped from 50 GJ to 5 GJ, and Defender Missiles now fly for 5 seconds instead of 3.

What about CONCORD beacons?

The minimum deployment distance from starbases increased from 30,000km to 50,000km for both the regular and carrier versions.

Where can I see the exact live numbers?

In the in-game module and ship info windows. The expansion notes are the source for this page, but CCP can adjust values with hotfixes.
