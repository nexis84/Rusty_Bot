How they work

Synchronizer Rigs are faction-focused rigs with a scaling bonus and a CPU cost.

Scale with modules

Each matching faction module adds a flat 1% to the rig's bonuses. A Caldari Navy Synchronizer grows with every Caldari Navy module fitted.

One per ship

Only one Synchronizer Rig can be fitted to a ship at a time.

Three sizes

Small, medium and large. Pick the size that fits your hull and rig slots.

Pay the CPU cost

Every Synchronizer Rig has a CPU drawback. Synchronization Rigging reduces the CPU penalty by 10% per level.

Synchronization Rigging

Train it to manage the CPU cost

The new rigging skill is available in the respective empire schools for 25 million ISK. Each level reduces the CPU penalty of Synchronizer Rigs.

  • 10% reduction in Synchronizer Rig CPU penalty per level
  • Level I required for Synchronizer Rig production
  • 25M ISK skill book in empire schools

Manufacturing

Build from salvage and wormhole materials

Synchronizer Rigs require Tech I salvage materials used by similar rigs, plus additional components.

  • Amarr, Caldari and Minmatar: additional components primarily from Class 1–3 wormhole content, such as Sleeper salvage and low-class gas reactions.
  • Gallente: rogue drone components from sentient commander NPCs, plus low-class wormhole gas reactions.
  • Pirate rigs: built from empire Synchronizer Rigs, based on the skills needed to fly that pirate faction's ships, plus low-class wormhole gas reaction components.

Why fit one

Synchronizer Rigs reward commitment to a faction's module line. The more matching modules you fit, the stronger the rig's bonuses become — but you give up a rig slot, pay a CPU cost, and in the case of pirate rigs, accept a significant penalty.

For fitting experimentation and faction-focused builds, that trade-off is the point. Nine sets cover the four empires and five pirate factions, with the pirate rigs offering bonuses no existing rig provides.

“Nine Synchronizer sets cover the four empires and five pirate factions, creating new room for fitting experimentation, theorycrafting, and faction-focused builds. Pirate Synchronizers bring bonuses that no existing rig offers, but come with heavier drawbacks.”

FAQ

What do Synchronizer Rigs do?

They provide a set of faction-focused bonuses that scale with matching faction modules. Each Synchronizer Rig also has a CPU drawback.

How do the bonuses scale?

All of the rig's bonuses increase by a flat 1% for every matching faction module fitted. For example, a Caldari Navy Synchronizer gets stronger with each Caldari Navy module.

Can I fit more than one?

No. Only one Synchronizer Rig can be fitted to a ship at a time.

What sizes are available?

Small, medium and large.

Where do I get them?

Empire rigs are in the Factional Warfare LP stores. Domination and Dread Guristas are in the Insurgency LP store. Dark Blood, True Sansha and Shadow Serpentis are in the regular pirate LP stores.

What is the drawback?

Every Synchronizer Rig has a CPU penalty. Pirate rigs add a significant faction-specific penalty on top of that. The Synchronization Rigging skill reduces the CPU penalty by 10% per level.

Do I need the Synchronization Rigging skill?

Level I is required for Synchronizer Rig production. Training the skill further reduces the rig CPU penalty by 10% per level. The skill book costs 25 million ISK in the respective empire schools.

How much LP and ISK do the BPCs cost?

The expansion notes do not list a fixed LP/ISK price for the rig BPCs. Check the relevant LP store for the current cost.
