Getting Started — 3 steps

  1. New to PI? Open Reference → Planets to see which planet makes what, then pick a product in Chain to see its full build.
  2. Have a home system? Use System Checker to see if it can build locally — or let Finder scan nearby systems for the most profitable build.
  3. Already have colonies? Login with EVE SSO in Colonies or Finder — expires & fill bars update from live ESI.

Sidebar — where the tools live

The left sidebar (like all other pages) holds the controls. On Welcome you can jump anywhere — the same tabs are available in the bottom toolbar too.

Tips

  • Click any material node in a chain to re-target it; double-click drills in.
  • In Finder, cards show a gate route — click again to expand it. Next Best is ranked by Jita and clickable.
  • Colonies: Idle only filters factories missing schematics/routes. Timeline shows expiry soonest-first.
  • Share a chain: the URL hash updates (#view=chain&product=...) — copy it.

EVE Online PI Visualizer — Chains, System Checker & Finder

Free, no-signup EVE Online Planetary Interaction tool. Browse every P0 raw → P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 production chain with exact inputs, batch sizes and planet types. Powered by the EVE SDE and live ESI market pricing across Jita (The Forge), Amarr (Domain), Dodixie, Rens, Hek and Manifest.

Chain view shows the full build down to raw extraction. System Checker uses offline SDE data: type any system to see its planets, skyhook power/workforce/reagents and which P2/P3/P4 are producible locally. Finder searches by gate jumps (1–50, via pi-jumps.js) and max systems (1–6) to surface full-coverage build plans — Scan Market ranks every producible product by Jita profit/batch & margin.

My Colonies is ESI-live: SSO (esi-planets, optional esi-skills/esi-location) pulls colony pins, launchpad/storage fill, extractor expiry countdowns, idle factories and ISK/day estimates. Link radius calibrates CPU/Powergrid to in-game values. Data respects ESI's 600s cache.
