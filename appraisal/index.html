    RustyAppraisal - EVE Online Item Appraisal Tool

    This free tool lets capsuleers paste a list of items from the EVE Online clipboard and instantly appraise their market value. Choose between the major trade hubs — Jita (The Forge), Amarr (Domain), Dodixie (Sinq Laison), Hek (Metropolis) and Rens (Heimatar) — or get a combined appraisal across all five hubs.

    For every item you'll see the best buy order price (the highest price buyers are currently willing to pay) and the best sell order price (the lowest price sellers are asking). Totals are shown for both, so you can quickly value a hangar, a loot drop or a shopping list in ISK.

    Prices are pulled live from CCP's ESI API and cached briefly, so repeated appraisals are fast while staying up to date.

    Looking for a single item instead? Browse live orders in the EVE Online Market Browser, learn the game with our EVE Online Guides, or return to the RustyBot homepage for more free tools.
