Inventory

How inventory search works

Enter your build system, pick a source, then Scan system. We pull every accessible item in that system — structures ESI can't resolve are excluded, never guessed. Industrial only limits the list to build materials (ore, compressed ore, ice, moon, gas, minerals, PI at your refining % from Calc > Rigs) plus industrial hulls (R.A.M., R.Db, haulers, barges, exhumers, Rorqual, Orca). Only the build materials go into the snapshot used for deducting and the Refinery — a hull is never a raw material. Use these deducts them from Shopping/Build/Mining and feeds the Refinery panel.
