Overview

Angel Refuge is an unrated combat site belonging to the Angel Cartel. It is found through exploration as a cosmic anomaly in systems owned by the Minmatar Republic or occupied by the Angel Cartel.

As an ungated complex, the practical ship limit follows the security rating where the anomaly spawns: up to T2 battleships in high-sec, and capitals in low-sec. The site is a single open pocket with a mix of frigate and destroyer waves plus fixed sentry batteries.

Auto-aggro site. Each wave aggros on entry or when the previous wave is destroyed. The last ship in a wave usually triggers the next spawn, so clear the field before moving on.

Escalation

Angel's Red Light District

Destroying the final wave can escalate this anomaly into the DED-rated Angel's Red Light District. The escalation is not guaranteed.

Loot & strategy

Notable loot

  • Domination faction modules and ammunition from the site's Angel Cartel ships.
  • The optional Domination Defacer can drop faction modules and tags.
  • Salvage from the frigate and destroyer wrecks.

Running it

  • Auto-aggro: the whole pocket can come at you, so watch the sentry batteries as well as the ships.
  • Ruffians apply a target painter — kill them early if your signature radius matters.
  • The final wave is optional; clearing it is what gives the escalation roll.
  • High-sec allows T2 battleships; low-sec opens the site up to capitals.
