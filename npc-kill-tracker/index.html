Rusty NPC Tracker — EVE Online ratting & bounty tracker

Every time you destroy an NPC in EVE Online the client writes a bounty line into your local game log. Rusty NPC Tracker connects to that log — no login required, and an optional read-only ESI link fills in the system when your log has no jump or undock line, plus shows your current ship and online status — and turns it into a live ratting dashboard: which NPC you killed, its bounty, the system it happened in, live incoming/outgoing DPS from the combat lines, and your ISK per hour in real time.

The NPC database is generated straight from CCP's official static data export (2,800+ bounty NPCs across New Eden), and kill locations are tracked from the jump and undock lines in the same log. Amount adjustments from ESS (Encounter Surveillance System), system bounty modifiers and event payout scalings are matched automatically, and you can fine-tune everything.

ISK per hour is tick-based: bounties pay out in 20-minute ticks at :07/:27/:47 UTC (three per hour), so the rate uses completed ticks only. The current partial tick is shown separately and is never mixed into the hourly figure.

Bounty amounts are only part of the picture — where several NPC types share a bounty, the tracker cross-references the NPCs you were actually fighting seconds before each kill for a confident match, and anything still ambiguous is one click to fix (optionally applied to every matching kill in the session).

Old sessions can be imported by dropping any number of game logs onto the page — perfect for comparing anomalies, systems or fits over weeks of ratting. All history is stored locally in your browser and can be exported to CSV or JSON.
