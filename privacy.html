1. Who we are

RustyBot (“RustyBot”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent project that provides an EVE Online Discord bot and website. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact us at sponsors@rustybot.co.uk or through the RustyBot Discord support server.

This policy covers the Discord bot, www.rustybot.co.uk, the shoutout dashboard and related APIs. It does not cover Discord, CCP, Ko-fi, Twitch, Kick or other third-party services, which have their own privacy policies.

2. What we collect

Discord data

  • Account and server data: Discord user ID, username and display name, avatar, server (guild) ID and name, channel and role IDs, and permissions where needed to run a command or show the dashboard.
  • Command and interaction data: which commands you use, the options you provide, button/select/modal interactions and responses. This can include item names, route systems, poll questions and options, poll votes, giveaway entries and winner data.
  • Server configuration: shoutout channels, alert roles, streamer lists, cooldowns, Ko-fi link status and similar settings chosen by server managers.
  • Content you submit: bug reports, patch notes, evidence links, d-scan text and other text you send through the bot. Bug reports and patch notes include your Discord tag and avatar so we can follow up. D-scan text is held in memory for about five minutes and then discarded; the resulting summary may be posted in the channel.
  • Giveaway winner details: if you win a giveaway, the in-game name (IGN) you submit and the public EVE character information looked up from that name (such as character ID, name and portrait) may be posted in the winner announcement channel.
  • Dashboard data: if you log in to the shoutout dashboard with Discord, we receive your Discord ID, username, avatar and the list of servers you can manage. The dashboard uses a session cookie to keep you logged in.

Ko-fi supporter data

  • Email address, Discord ID (if you link one), Ko-fi payment/transaction ID, tier, timestamps, expiry and status.
  • We do not receive or store your full card or bank details; those are handled by Ko-fi and its payment providers.

Twitch and Kick data

  • Streamer login names, live/offline status and stream metadata used to post shoutouts.
  • API access and refresh tokens needed to check stream status. These are stored on our servers and are not shared with other users.

EVE Online data

  • Public character ID and name returned by CCP’s EVE ESI when you use an EVE SSO feature on the website.
  • EVE SSO access and refresh tokens are returned to your browser and stored in your browser’s localStorage so you can stay signed in. We do not intentionally store your EVE SSO tokens on our servers; the token exchange is transient. Clearing your browser storage logs you out.
  • Market, route, kill and LP data is public EVE data and is not linked to your account unless you include it in a command, report or other submission.

Website, analytics and logs

  • IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location and timestamps collected by our hosting providers and Google Analytics 4.
  • Command usage events sent from the Discord bot to Google Analytics 4. These events may include the command name, your Discord username, and server/channel IDs.
  • Server logs containing requests, errors, IP addresses and Discord IDs for security, debugging and abuse prevention.

3. Cookies and local storage

  • Google Analytics 4: the website sets _ga and _ga_* cookies to measure usage. You can block or delete them in your browser, or use Google’s opt-out browser add-on.
  • Dashboard session: the shoutout dashboard sets an HTTP-only session cookie named shout_discord_session so you stay logged in.
  • Local storage: website tools store EVE SSO tokens, character ID/name and preferences in your browser’s localStorage. These stay on your device until you clear them.

4. Why we use your data

  • To provide the bot and website features you request (performance of a contract or your request).
  • To remember server settings, giveaway entries and supporter access (performance of a contract or legitimate interests).
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and debug problems (legitimate interests).
  • To understand feature usage and improve the Service (legitimate interests, or consent where required).
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Where we rely on consent (for example, non-essential analytics cookies where required), you can withdraw it at any time.

5. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. In general:

  • D-scan text: about five minutes (in memory).
  • Server configurations and supporter state: until the server is removed, the feature is disabled, or you ask us to delete it.
  • Giveaway entries and poll state: until the giveaway or poll ends and is no longer needed for rerolls, support or records.
  • Logs: for a limited period for security and debugging, then deleted or overwritten.
  • Ko-fi payment records: for as long as needed to verify supporter access and meet accounting or tax obligations.

Some content posted in Discord (messages, bug reports, patch notes, giveaway announcements) is stored by Discord under Discord’s own policies and may remain after we delete our copy.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Discord — to receive interactions and send messages.
  • CCP / EVE ESI, zKillboard, EVE Ref and Fuzzwork — to fetch public EVE data.
  • Ko-fi — to process voluntary support and verify supporter access.
  • Twitch and Kick — to check stream status for shoutouts.
  • Google Analytics — for website and command usage analytics.
  • Hosting providers — Render (website) and Oracle Cloud (bot/dashboard), which process data on our behalf.
  • Legal and safety — if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a merger or reorganisation.

Some providers are outside the UK or EEA (for example, in the United States). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit, access controls and limited retention. No service is completely secure. Please do not submit passwords, API keys or other secrets through the bot.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at sponsors@rustybot.co.uk with your Discord user ID and, if relevant, server ID. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. If you are in the EEA, you can complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. You must meet Discord’s minimum age (and any higher age required in your country) to use Discord. If you are under 18, use the Service with a parent or guardian’s permission. If we learn that we have collected data from a child who is too young, we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

The Service may link to third-party sites and services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change, and material changes will be announced in the support server or on the website where reasonable.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: sponsors@rustybot.co.uk, or join the RustyBot Discord support server.
