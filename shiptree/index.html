Getting Started — 3 steps

  1. New to EVE? Start with the Combat or Industry filter, then wander the faction lanes. Click any hull for stats, bonuses and mastery.
  2. Chasing a ship? Use Search, open the hull, and read the Skills panel for the exact prerequisites and training order.
  3. Already flying? Sign in with EVE SSO — the tree highlights what you can undock now and what's still left to train.

Tips

  • Press / to search, Esc to clear. Arrow keys step between hulls once you've selected one.
  • Drag the canvas to pan, scroll to zoom, and use + / − / ⊞ to zoom or fit to screen.
  • Click a hull then Compare to pin a second column — ideal for picking your next ship.
  • Set your attributes to sharpen the training-time estimates.
  • Planning the build too? Open the Blueprint Visualizer right from a hull's panel.

EVE Online Ship Tree — Hulls, Skills & Jita Prices

Free, no-signup EVE Online ship tree. Browse every published hull — frigates to titans, Tech I to faction and pirate — organised into faction lanes and hull-class rows. Each hull shows the skills it requires, Alpha-clone access, mastery levels, bonuses and an estimated Jita price.

Use Search for any hull or skill, or filter to combat and industry hulls, Alpha-flyable ships, what you can't fly yet, or hulls not yet at Mastery V. The skills panel explains every prerequisite and builds a training plan, and the compare view lines two hulls up side by side.

Sign in with EVE SSO to colour the whole tree against your real trained skills and see at a glance what you can undock right now.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EVE Online Ship Tree free?
Yes — free to browse with no signup. Signing in with EVE SSO is optional and highlights what your character can fly right now.

What does Alpha access mean on a hull?
Hulls flyable by Alpha clone pilots are flagged, so new players can see what they can fly on a free account.

Where do the Jita prices come from?
Estimated prices come live from EVE Online's ESI market data for Jita.

Does it show skill requirements?
Yes — every hull lists prerequisite skills and a training order, with training-time estimates based on your attributes.
