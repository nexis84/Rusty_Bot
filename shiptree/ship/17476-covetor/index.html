Description

The mining barge was designed by ORE to facilitate advancing the mining profession to a new level. Each barge was created to excel at a specific function, the Covetor's being mining yield and mining laser range. This additional yield comes at a price, as the Covetor has weaker defenses and a smaller ore bay than the other mining barges.

Mining barges are equipped with electronic subsystems specifically designed to accommodate Strip Mining and Ice Harvesting modules.
