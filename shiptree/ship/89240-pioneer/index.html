Description

Towards the end of YC127, new frontiers of opportunity opened up for the miners of New Eden. To help them rise to meet these opportunities, ORE’s design division began work on a reliable and effective ship that could outperform the Venture without the demands of other, more advanced, vessels.

The result was the Pioneer, a ship designed to extend the reach of the nascent miner as they build towards ever greater success.
