Description

Faced with the prospect of maintaining order in Syndicate in YC128, a region beyond the reach of CONCORD, the Caldari Navy needed a new hard-hitting solution that would establish State authority through its intimidating presence alone. The Naga Navy Issue was commissioned in partnership with the megacorporations of the Mountain faction to ensure that the newly controlled region would witness the uncompromisable honor and strength of the Caldari.
