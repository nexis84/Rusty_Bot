Description

In YC128, as the stability of Pochven wavered, the Blood Raiders saw an opportunity to plunder the region for the secrets of Triglavian biotech. The Akoman was constructed to help them take advantage of that opportunity.

Facing off against an inarguably more advanced foe, the Blood Raiders needed a vessel that represented the best of their own technical prowess. This attack battlecruiser allowed Blood Raider fleets to form stronger frontline assaults as they struck at the heart of Triglavian territory.
