How it works

Same idea on every setup — connect, choose streamers, and let the alerts run.

1

Open a dashboard

Open the Twitch/Kick or Discord dashboard and connect your channel. Support via Ko-fi is welcomed to keep it free.

2

Choose your streamers

Start from the shared streamer list and enable the ones you want. Mark favourites as priority.

3

Frequently asked

The short answers before you connect anything.

Is this only for EVE Online streamers?

No. The shared EVE streamer list is just a starting point — you can add or import the channels you want to follow, and enable or disable individual streamers per server.

Does Kick work?

Yes. The Twitch / Kick ShoutOut posts to both platforms, and you can link separate Twitch and Kick logins for the same streamer group.

Is RustyBot ShoutOuts free?

Yes. Twitch/Kick and Discord ShoutOuts are both free, with all current features included. Support via Ko-fi is welcomed to keep it free.

Do I need a Ko-fi membership?

No. Ko-fi support is optional and does not change access. If you already support on Ko-fi, you keep a supporter badge in the dashboard.

How fast are the alerts?

RustyBot checks tracked streamers about every 60 seconds and posts each live stream once, with a per-streamer cooldown so channels don't flood.

How can I support the project?

If you'd like to help with hosting and development, you can donate on Ko-fi. Donations are optional and never required for access.

Do I need my own bot?

No. Sign in and link your channel on the Twitch/Kick dashboard. For Discord, invite RustyBot and run /shoutout setup in your server.

Ready to switch on live alerts?

Connect your first streamer in a few minutes — no code and no bot hosting.
