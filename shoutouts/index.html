Twitch & Discord ShoutOuts

Automatic live alerts for your chat and your community

RustyBot ShoutOuts watch your tracked streamers and post an alert the moment they go live. There are two versions: one for Twitch & Kick chat, and one for Discord servers. Both use the same shared streamer list and the same Ko-fi membership — pick the home that suits your audience.

Choose your platform

Both versions run from their own dashboard and set up in a few minutes.

For Twitch & Kick streamers

Twitch / Kick ShoutOut

Alerts post straight into Twitch or Kick chat whenever a tracked streamer goes live — perfect for watch parties, raid targets and community channels.

  • Tracks the shared streamer list
  • Posts to Twitch and Kick chat automatically
  • Priority streamers can use a separate channel
  • Per-streamer cooldown stops repeat spam
  • Dashboard commands, timers and custom messages

Included with AA Battery Supplier or higher on Ko-fi.

For Discord communities

Discord ShoutOut

RustyBot posts a live alert in your Discord server when a tracked streamer goes live, with the Twitch link and an optional role ping.

  • Set the announcement channel with /shoutout setup
  • Enable or disable each known streamer
  • Priority streamers get their own channel
  • One membership per Discord server
  • Works alongside the RustyBot Discord bot

Included with Bot Whisperer or Elite Supporter Status on Ko-fi.

Open Discord ShoutOut

How it works

Same idea on both platforms — connect, choose streamers, and let the alerts run.

1

Become a member

Pick a qualifying Ko-fi tier, then link the same email (or your Discord ID) in the dashboard.

2

Choose your streamers

Start from the shared streamer list and enable the ones you want. Mark favourites as priority.

3

Alerts post automatically

RustyBot checks every 60 seconds and posts each live stream once, with a cooldown so channels don't flood.
