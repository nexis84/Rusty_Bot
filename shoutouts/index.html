Frequently asked

The short answers before you connect anything.

Is this only for EVE Online streamers?

No. The shared EVE streamer list is just a starting point — you can add or import the channels you want to follow, and enable or disable individual streamers per server.

Does Kick work?

Yes. The Twitch / Kick ShoutOut posts to both platforms, and you can link separate Twitch and Kick logins for the same streamer group.

Can one membership cover both platforms?

Yes — Bot Whisperer and Elite Supporter Status qualify for both. Link the same Ko-fi email in each dashboard. AA Battery Supplier covers Twitch/Kick only.

How fast are the alerts?

RustyBot checks tracked streamers about every 60 seconds and posts each live stream once, with a per-streamer cooldown so channels don't flood.

What happens if I cancel?

Your ShoutOut stays active until the end of the paid period. After that, alerts pause until you renew a qualifying tier.

Do I need my own bot?

No. Sign in and link your channel on the Twitch/Kick dashboard. For Discord, invite RustyBot and run /shoutout setup in your server.
