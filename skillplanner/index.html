Rusty Skill Planner About, FAQ & tools
Rusty Skill Planner

Rusty Skill Planner is a free browser tool for planning EVE Online skill training. Search any skill, build a training queue, and let the planner add prerequisites, order the plan and estimate every step from your character's attributes.

Free to use EVE SSO optional SDE-powered
Is Rusty Skill Planner free?

Yes — free in your browser. No account is needed to plan; EVE SSO sign-in simply tailors the plan to your character.

Does it include prerequisites?

Yes — when you add a skill, its prerequisite skills are added to the queue automatically in training order.

Can it calculate skill injectors?

Yes — enter your current SP, target SP and an injector price to see how many injectors you need and the ISK cost. Large skill injector prices are loaded from the Jita market.

Can I import or export a skill plan?

Yes — paste an EVE skill plan or choose a .json/.txt file, copy a plan for EVE's in-game import, download it as .txt, or share it with a link.

Can I import a ship fit?

Yes — paste an EFT fit from EVE. The planner resolves the hull, modules, drones and charges, works out the skills you are missing, and creates a plan you can export back to EVE.

Does it check my saved EVE fittings?

Yes — sign in with EVE SSO to load your personal saved fittings, see which ones you can fly, how long the missing skills will take, and create a training plan for each fit.

Can I save corporation or alliance fits?

Yes — ESI only exposes your personal fittings, so paste corp or alliance EFT fits into the Doctrine Library. They are stored locally in your browser and can be combined into a doctrine training plan.
