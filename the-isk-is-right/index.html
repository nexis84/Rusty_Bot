About The ISK is Right! — the free EVE Online price game

The ISK is Right! is a free price-guessing game for EVE Online. Every round shows you two ships and you decide which one costs more at Jita's market. Pick right to keep your streak alive and grow your ISK reward; pick wrong and you lose a life — you can even buy lives back with the ISK you've earned.

Prices are fetched live from EVE Online's ESI market data for Jita (The Forge), so the game is always in step with the real player-run economy. It's a great way to sharpen your market knowledge of hull prices, from frigates to titans.

Are the prices real?

Yes — game prices come directly from ESI's Jita market data, the same source that powers our EVE Online market browser.

What happens when I run out of lives?

You can buy extra lives using the ISK rewards you've collected, or start a fresh run.

Do I need an EVE account?

No — the game is playable by anyone in a modern browser, free and without login.
