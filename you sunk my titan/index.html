You Sunk My Titan

EVE Online battleship — 10×10, mono-faction fleets, vs AI only. Scan with your frigate, doomsday with your titan.

Pick a faction and difficulty, then place your fleet on the left board — click to place each ship (R = rotate, Randomise for auto). Then fire at Space on the right. Frigate (2) has a 1× 3×3 Tactical Scan (reveal, no damage). Titan (6) unlocks a 1× Doomsday full row/column strike after taking first damage.
